HOW TO PLAY // PI-SECT

Infinite-Precision Binary & Quadtree Search

01.
Read the Crosshairs: Check which side of the 50% (½) primary crosshair the orb is in. Faded 2° (¼, ¾) & 3° (⅛..⅞) lines let you plan 2–3 cuts ahead!
02.
Beat the Step Clock: Slice into the target region before the collapsing ring shifts Cyan → Amber → Red.
03.
3 Lives (❤️) & Zoom: Every cut zooms in (2× in 1D, 4× in 2D) to lock in digits of π, φ, √2. Mistakes or timeouts cost 1 life.
